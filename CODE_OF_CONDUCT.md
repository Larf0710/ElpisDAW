# ElpisDAW Community Code of Conduct

## Status

This policy defines the expected behavior for future ElpisDAW community spaces.
Public contribution intake and community moderation are not open until a
private conduct-reporting channel is approved and published.

## Our Standard

ElpisDAW welcomes people who participate in good faith, regardless of
experience, background, identity, role, employer, or preferred tools.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and code without attacking people;
- giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- respecting different levels of experience and access needs;
- protecting private Projects, identities, credentials, and unreleased work;
- crediting contributions and third-party material accurately;
- accepting a clear no when a request crosses a safety, licensing, or scope
  boundary; and
- helping keep the project useful to creators rather than turning discussion
  into advertising or harassment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- personal insults, intimidation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information or private creative work;
- knowingly submitting malware, credentials, proprietary code, model weights,
  or unlicensed assets;
- impersonating ElpisDAW maintainers or presenting a fork, Provider, or
  Extension as official without authorization;
- manipulating issue, review, or moderation processes in bad faith; or
- retaliating against someone who makes a good-faith safety or conduct report.

## Scope

This policy applies in official ElpisDAW repositories, issue trackers, review
threads, community spaces, events, and private project communications. It also
applies when a person publicly represents the project or acts with project
authority.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to
protect the community and project.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and separated from
technical disagreement. Reports should be shared only with the people needed to
evaluate and resolve them.

Do not submit a conduct report through a public issue. Before public community
spaces open, the project must publish a private moderation channel and an appeal
route. Until then, external community and contribution intake remains closed.
